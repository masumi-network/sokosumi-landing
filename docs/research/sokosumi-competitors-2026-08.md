# Sokosumi competitor landscape — August 2026

Research date: 2026-08-25. Sources are the companies' own sites, docs and press releases wherever they were reachable; where a vendor's pricing page blocked fetching, the price is taken from a named third-party page and flagged as such. "Not published" means no source states it.

## 0. Sokosumi baseline (what we are comparing against)

- Marketplace where marketing teams hire named AI coworkers (Elena/strategy, Hannah/research, Alex/coding, Maya/creative) and single-purpose agents; brief in plain language or @mention in a channel; shared task board (Backlog → Queued → In Progress → Input Required → Done); output is finished files (reports, decks, spreadsheets, dashboards). Every coworker "built and operated by a named vendor" with a public profile that discloses model and hosting region. EU hosting available; product page shows Claude + Mistral models on "Azure · Frankfurt". Legal entity Plan.Net Germany GmbH & Co KG, Munich; built by Serviceplan Group with NMKR. Sources: https://www.sokosumi.com/about , https://www.sokosumi.com/product
- Pricing: Free €0 / 250 credits per seat; Starter €25 / 1,500; Standard €75 / 5,000; Pro €200 / 15,000; Enterprise custom. https://www.sokosumi.com/pricing
- History: marketplace launched 25 June 2025 (https://campaigngermany.de/news/beitrag/1002-sokosumi-serviceplan-launcht-marktplatz-fuer-ki-agenten.html); "Hannah" and "Elena" AI Coworkers announced Feb/Mar 2026 as the first SME offer from Serviceplan's House of AI (https://www.house-of-communication.com/de/de/newsroom/2026/02/serviceplan-group-ai-coworker.html). Sister front door serviceplan-agents.com lists the same five tiers (Free tier there shows 200 credits vs 250 on sokosumi.com — worth aligning) (http://www.serviceplan-agents.com/).

## 1. Direct: AI coworker / agent-workforce platforms for business teams

### Langdock (Berlin)
- What: enterprise AI workspace (chat, custom agents, workflows) with 40+ models, EU-hosted, ISO 27001/SOC 2. https://langdock.com/pricing
- Targets: whole-company rollouts (up to 1,000 users on Business; Enterprise beyond), DACH/EU mid-market and enterprise.
- Pricing (published): Business €25/user/month (Standard seat) or €99/user/month (Business Max, 5x usage); 7-day trial with €5 model credits; Workflows add-on €539/month (40k runs) or €1,199/month (100k runs) per workspace; Governance add-on free until 1 Jan 2027, then €3.50/user/month; API usage-based per million tokens. https://langdock.com/pricing
- Funding/size: $3.5M total seed (General Catalyst, La Famiglia, YC); Sacra estimates $42M ARR June 2026. https://sacra.com/c/langdock/ , https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/19/langdock/
- Overlap vs difference: same price point as Sokosumi Starter (€25) and same EU-hosting story, so it is the most common "we already have Langdock" objection in DACH. Langdock sells a build-your-own agent workspace for every employee; Sokosumi sells pre-built, vendor-operated marketing coworkers that return finished files. No marketplace, no named vendors, no task board.

### Lindy (San Francisco)
- What: no-code "AI employees"/agents for email, meetings, CRM, outreach; relaunched 2026 as an AI executive assistant. https://www.lindy.ai/pricing

- Pricing (published): Plus $29.99/user/month (3,000 credits), Pro $99.99 (15,000), Max $199.99 (35,000); Enterprise custom; 7-day trial, no free plan. https://www.lindy.ai/pricing

## Positioning gaps

Competitors claim, Sokosumi does not (yet) say:
- Deep integrations in the user's stack (Viktor 3,000+ tools, Coworker 50+ connectors, Lindy/Relevance 2,000+) — Sokosumi's story is brief → files, not living inside CRM/Drive.
- Build-your-own agents and governance/approval workflows (Langdock, Dust, Writer, Relevance).
- Outcome-based pricing (HubSpot pays per resolved outcome; Google marketplace supports outcome pricing).
- Scale proof (Langdock $42M ARR, Sintra 40k customers, Viktor 2,000 orgs).
- Free tiers with recurring credits (Genspark 100/day, Manus 300/day) vs Sokosumi's flat 250.

Sokosumi claims that competitors do not:
- Multi-vendor marketplace where each coworker names its builder/operator and discloses model and hosting region.
- Finished files (PDF, deck, sheet, live dashboard) as the unit of work, with visible credit price before running.
- Agency lineage (Serviceplan) plus premium research data partners (Statista, GWI, dpa, DataForSEO).
