### The "House of Communication" model
- Fully integrated agency model: all communication disciplines (brand strategists, creatives, media, data scientists, tech) under one roof, per location. Serviceplan calls the culture "Houses of Communication"; London got a full HoC in 2025 (Mediaplus, Plan.Net, Serviceplan, Silverside, Ace of Hearts; Total Media integrated as Mediaplus UK). Source: https://www.house-of-communication.com/de/en/newsroom/2025/07/serviceplan-group-2024-2025-fiscal-year.html ; https://www.house-of-communication.com/int/en.html
- The Munich HQ moved to a new House of Communication in the Werksviertel (activity-based working). Source: https://www.house-of-communication.com/int/en/newsroom/twelve-mail/twelve-mail-no-8/interview-florian-haller.html
- Tagline: "ÜberCreativity" / "Building Best Brands" (Haller). Source: https://www.house-of-communication.com/int/en.html

## 2. AI strategy

### 2.1 "House of AI" — the umbrella
- **Definition (official):** "the digital twin of our House of Communication … a connected operating system" combining human expertise and AI across the marketing value chain. Source: https://www.house-of-communication.com/de/en/solutions/artificial-intelligence.html
- **Structure:** Foundation = fully compliant **Global Data Platform** + **Plus.AI** intelligence layer. Four solution suites: **Insight.AI** (real-time audience/behaviour/journey intelligence), **Creative.AI** (scalable personalised content), **Activate.AI** (real-time activation, optimisation, media performance), **Agentic.AI** (specialised AI agents, client-specific ecosystems on demand). Source: same page; German version https://www.house-of-communication.com/de/de/solutions/artificial-intelligence.html describes "three central pillars" (Insight/Creative/Activate) plus Agentic.AI and the Global Data Platform.
- W&V (2025-12-11) describes the org chart as "a house: foundation, three pillars, roof"; "ein digitaler Zwilling des etablierten Serviceplan-Ökosystems"; framed as Serviceplan's first comprehensive AI system for the German market, built from years of reinvestment. Source: https://www.wuv.de/Themen/Agentur/Das-ist-die-KI-Strategie-von-Serviceplan (paywalled preview)
- Insight.AI sub-modules named on LinkedIn: **Research.AI** (AI surveys/focus groups), **Audience.AI** (segmentation), **Persona.AI** (interactive personas). Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/serviceplan-group_house-of-ai-x-insightai-activity-7445356874616598528--wyR (≈April 2026)
- Guiding principle: "**Augmented Intelligence**. Technology does not replace people. It empowers them." Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/serviceplan-group_house-of-ai-activity-7434206227959685120-TV3X (≈March 2026)
- Who fronts it: **Matthias Brüll** (Global CEO Mediaplus) is the quoted leader on the House of AI page; **Felix Bartels** is the sales contact. Source: https://www.house-of-communication.com/de/en/solutions/artificial-intelligence.html
- FY 2025/26 strategic priorities listed by the group: (1) Mediaplus global expansion, (2) London, (3) **House of AI implementation**. Source: https://www.house-of-communication.com/int/en/newsroom/2026/07/serviceplan-group-2025-2026-fiscal-year.html
- No named "Chief AI Officer" for the group was found. **[UNVERIFIED]** whether such a role exists.
