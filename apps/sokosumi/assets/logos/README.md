# Brand marks

`models/` and `infra/` hold third-party brand marks taken from
[simple-icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), which is
CC0-1.0 (public domain). They are used nominatively — to identify the model
a coworker names on its listing, and the infrastructure Sokosumi runs on.

`openai.svg` is deliberately absent: simple-icons does not carry it, and the
page falls back to setting the name in type rather than approximating a
trademark. `models/grok.svg` is the xAI (X) mark, since simple-icons has no
separate Grok icon.

`eu.svg` is drawn from the emblem's published construction (12 stars on a
circle of radius h/3, #003399 field, #FFCC00 stars), not copied.
