Hero background lab: test.mp4

The same clip under five treatments. Each frame is the live landing page, so the motion is real. Click a heading to open that variant full-screen.

1 · Cinematic (current treatment)

The existing scrim: dark left-to-right and top/bottom gradients, colors of the clip shining through. Closest to Harvey's own hero.

2 · Ink wash

Flat, heavy dark overlay. The clip becomes a faint texture; maximum text focus, most minimal.

3 · Monochrome

Grayscale with the cinematic scrim. Matches the grayscale Serviceplan photo and the ink chapters; the whole page becomes one palette.

4 · Purple duotone

Grayscale clip washed in Wisteria purple. The strongest brand statement, and the most color.

5 · Frosted

Heavy blur turns the clip into slow-moving ambient color. Abstract, calm, no legibility fight at all.
