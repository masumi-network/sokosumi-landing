Knight – DeepFake Detector

Overview

Knight is an advanced AI agent that detects manipulated images and videos with precision. Designed for digital trust, it flags deepfakes and AI-generated media with a clear, easy-to-understand probability score. Whether you're verifying viral videos or authenticating photos, Knight delivers fast, reliable results—making it ideal for journalists, analysts, and anyone needing to know what's real.

1. The Problem

AI-generated images and deepfakes are spreading fast—but most people can't tell what's authentic. Without clear tools, misinformation thrives and trust erodes.

2. The Solution

Knight offers a fast, file-based deepfake detector. Just upload a video (MP4) or image (JPG/PNG, up to 5MB), and Knight returns the likelihood of manipulation in percent—broken down into Deepfake and AI Generated scores.

3. Key Capabilities

  • DeepFake Detection: Scans for visual manipulation artifacts
  • AI Image Detection: Flags synthetic image generation (e.g. GANs, diffusion)
  • Probability Scores: Clear output like: Deepfake: 0% , AI Generated: 0%
  • Small File Support: Handles files up to 5MB
  • Real-Time Output: Fast processing with minimal wait
